
How does "goto" work in our anim scripting language, i.e. src/anim.[ch] and
src/hexgame_state.[ch]?..

First of all, what are the top-level places where scripting language stuff is
kicked off?.. let's assume we can find that by finding all places where
state_effect_apply is called:

    state_effect_apply:
        parse_and_execute_effects
            NOTE: this is the -e|--effect CLI option
        _test_app_command_apply_effect
        body_execute_procs:
            body_step:
                body_execute_procs(body, STATESET_PROC_TYPE_FRAME_START)
                body_execute_procs(body, STATESET_PROC_TYPE_FRAME_END)
            body_move_to_map:
                body_execute_procs(body, STATESET_PROC_TYPE_MAP_CHANGE)
            body_set_stateset:
                body_execute_procs(body, STATESET_PROC_TYPE_STATESET_CHANGE)
            hexgame_load:
                body_execute_procs(body, STATESET_PROC_TYPE_STATESET_CHANGE)
            body_play_recording:
                body_execute_procs(body, STATESET_PROC_TYPE_STATESET_CHANGE)
            hexmap_load_hexmap_recording:
                body_execute_procs(body, STATESET_PROC_TYPE_STATESET_CHANGE)
        state_cond_match:
            state_cond_match (cycle)
            state_effect_apply (cycle)
            state_rule_match:
                state_handle_rules (see below)
        state_effect_apply (cycle)
        _call_proc:
            state_cond_match (see above)
            state_effect_apply (cycle)
        _apply_sub_effects:
            state_effect_apply (cycle)
        collmsg_handler_apply:
            body_collide_against_body:
                hexmap_step
        state_rule_apply:
            state_handle_rules:
                body_handle_rules:
                    body_step
                    collmsg_handler_apply (see above)
                        body_handle_rules called after state_effect_goto_apply_to_body
                    _apply_sub_effects (see above)
                        body_handle_rules called after state_effect_goto_apply_to_body
                    _test_app_command_apply_effect
                        body_handle_rules called after state_effect_goto_apply_to_body
                actor_handle_rules:
                    actor_step
                    collmsg_handler_apply (see above)
                        actor_handle_rules called after state_effect_goto_apply_to_actor

The effects of state_effect_goto_apply_to_body are:
* Set body->state
* Possibly update body->cooldown (for "goto delay")

The effects of state_effect_goto_apply_to_actor are:
* Set actor->state
* Possibly update actor->wait (for "goto delay")

And what about "goto immediate"?.. by the way, this is the goto structure:

    typedef struct state_effect_goto {
        const char *name;
        bool immediate;
        bool delay;
        int add_delay;
    } state_effect_goto_t;
